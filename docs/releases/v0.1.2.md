Every setting now opens inside Next Launcher, in the app's own look, instead of a separate "Epic Games" window.

- Country, email, password and two-factor, connected accounts, privacy, communication preferences, wallet, transactions, payment methods, gifts, codes, help and legal pages open right inside Settings. Use the back button or Escape to return.
- These pages wear the app's design: no website header or footer, the app's cards, buttons, fields and accent colour, a loading spinner instead of half-built pages, and no browser menus or shortcuts.
- Account pages open faster: one step instead of a chain of sign-in redirects.
- The app's license and the open-source notices open in the app instead of Notepad.
- Epic may sometimes ask you to confirm you're human or to verify a change; that check appears in the same place.
