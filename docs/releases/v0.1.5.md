Lots more animation across Next Launcher.

- Settings and the Store's categories (Featured, Free games, On sale, New releases) have a highlight that glides to what you pick.
- Pages build in: a Settings page's cards come in one after another, and a game's store page builds from the top, with its picture settling into place. Dialogs and menus bring their contents in too.
- While the store or a game's details load, you see shimmering placeholders instead of "Loading…".
- A game's status (Ready, Playing, Update available), your game count, the sync status, your wallet balance and the download status give a small bump when they change.
- Picking a new accent colour blends the whole app into it. Messages show a line that runs out while they're on screen.
- Buttons and icons react to the pointer: the gear turns, the wishlist heart beats, arrows lean, switches stretch while pressed, and games lift a little higher.
- As before, you need Settings › General › Appearance › Animations set to On if Windows' animation effects are off. With animations off, everything appears at once.
