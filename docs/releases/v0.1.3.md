Getting games, the wishlist, the cart and signing in now happen inside Next Launcher. There is no separate "Epic Games" window any more.

- Get and Buy now show Epic's checkout right inside the Store. You review and confirm there, as before; use the back button or Escape to return to the game. Once you've got it, the game shows "In your library".
- "View on Epic's store", Wishlist and Cart open inside the Store too, in the app's colours, without the website's own header and bars.
- Signing in shows Epic's sign-in page inside Settings › Account. Going back cancels it; once you're signed in you return to where you started.
- If Epic asks you to confirm something about your account, that page opens in the same place.
- Epic's payment step and its "are you human" checks are left exactly as Epic made them.
