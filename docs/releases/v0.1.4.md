Next Launcher now moves: pages, menus and cards animate instead of just switching.

- The section tabs and the library filters slide their highlight to what you pick. Pages fade up into place, and Settings opens with a slight zoom.
- Changing the library filter or sort brings your games in one after another; store cards do the same as they load. Cover art fades in as it loads and zooms a little under the pointer.
- Dialogs, menus, the wallet and profile menus, and messages now close smoothly too. Quick Launch games and the download card slide in and out, and a running download's bar has a moving light.
- "Playing" has a softly pulsing dot.
- New setting: Settings › General › Appearance › Animations. Match Windows (default) shows no motion when Windows' "Animation effects" are off; choose On to see animations anyway, or Off to turn them off in Next Launcher only.
- Nothing animates while the app starts or while you scroll the library, so it stays as fast as before.
